02 Good questions, answered

first call.

Every engagement is shaped around your business. Here’s a little more context before we get into the details.

Can you integrate with our existing tools?

We start with the systems already in use and assess what can connect securely and reliably before we recommend a build.

Will a workflow run without human review?

Only when the task and risk make that appropriate. We design human checkpoints where review or approval adds important assurance.

Can we start with one small workflow?

Yes. A focused pilot is often the clearest way to learn what works and where automation can create value.
